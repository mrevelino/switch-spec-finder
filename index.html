— of — Cisco Catalyst, HPE Aruba CX, Juniper EX and Fortinet FortiSwitch models meet the requirements below.

Checked on 13 Nov 2026 against the Cisco datasheets, HPE Aruba QuickSpecs, HPE-hosted Juniper datasheets and Fortinet FortiSwitch datasheets linked in each row. FortiSwitch rows are partly verified because Fortinet lists routing and MACsec features per family, not per model, and FortiSwitches are managed from a FortiGate (FortiLink) rather than stacked. The Verified column says how much of a row was confirmed, and a dash means the datasheet doesn't state that value. Juniper EX4300 is no longer published, so its rows are unverified. Aruba has no CX 6001, 6002 or 6003; the CX 6000 family is the 6000, 6100, 6200, 6300 and 6400 series, all listed here.
